## Top Stories

### [YC open-sources a shared agent harness](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49126604)
YC released an open-source harness for shared, always-on workplace agents, with per-user scopes, shared rooms, and deployment inside the operator's cloud account. Practitioners said the useful pattern is webhook-driven work such as CI repair and incident triage. The hard problem is controlling shared context, not the agent loop.

### [Elevator scheduling is a workload problem](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49124218)
An interactive simulator compares collective control, estimated wait-time routing, and destination dispatch, showing that frequent rebalancing can beat supposedly smarter kiosks. Commenters found the benchmark's weak spot: uniformly random trips ignore office traffic that naturally batches riders by floor. The lesson is broader than elevators: realistic workloads dominate clever schedulers.

### [Visible AI reasoning may be a useful fiction](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49124358)
Research reviewed by Quanta suggests visible chain-of-thought can be unfaithful: models retain performance even when trained on noisy or irrelevant traces. OpenAI claims current models fixed earlier failures but does not publish raw reasoning traces. The strongest objection is empirical: verified novel proofs still require more than plausible imitation, whatever the internal mechanism.

### [Go proposes a standard generic collections library](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49127031)
The Go team proposes standard generic collection types, finally addressing gaps such as typed sets and heaps without pushing generic machinery into application code. The thread's fairest defense of the delay: Go rejected designs that threatened compile speed, then evolved without breaking Go 1 compatibility.
