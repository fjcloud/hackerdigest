## Top Stories

### [14 military commissary freezers entered defrost mode simultaneously](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49508506)
Across 14 bases including a nuclear-ICBM base, internet-controlled freezers started defrost cycles at ~2AM and spoiled frozen food. A remote-management vulnerability was disclosed the same day. Thread consensus: probably a misconfigured update rather than state actors, but the real lesson is that military food storage should not be remotely controllable at all.

### [Darling brings experimental GUI support to macOS-on-Linux](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49515830)
The long-running Darwin compatibility layer for Linux now runs basic graphical apps, up from command-line only. The more interesting thread detail: Apple quietly introduced an entitlement letting ARM64 WINE/CrossOver bypass Rosetta, ahead of Rosetta's death in macOS 28.
