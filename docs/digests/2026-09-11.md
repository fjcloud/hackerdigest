## Top Stories

### [Shopify drops React Native, returns to Swift and Kotlin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643982)
Coding agents now make maintaining two native codebases affordable, Shopify argues. Commenters split between "this is the new norm" and "another swing of the cross-platform pendulum." One engineer pushed back: the post has no cost data, and AI makes RN development cheaper too.

### [Rust becomes tier-1 language at Microsoft](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643546)
Microsoft joins Google, Meta, and Amazon in promoting Rust internally, accompanied by rustc_codegen_utc, an MSVC backend that performs on par with LLVM. Commenters said C++ interop remains the hard problem: boundary code stays unsafe and idiomatic C++ rarely maps cleanly into Rust.

### [Cognition launches SWE-2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645443)
SWE-2 is post-trained from Kimi K3 and claims parity with Fable 5.1 and GPT-Astra. Commenters flagged the Terminal-Bench 2.1 score of 92.8% collapsing to 27.3% on Terminal-Bench 4 as evidence of benchmark fitting.

### [Forgejo critical RCE patched in 16.0.4](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645907)
Malicious repo templates can exploit variable template expansion to inject a `.git` folder and achieve RCE on self-hosted Forgejo. The 15.0.8 LTS release also includes the fix. Self-hosters with open registration should upgrade immediately.
