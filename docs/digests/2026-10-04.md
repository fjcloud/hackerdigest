## Top Stories

### [We're going to need default hard budget caps on pretty much everything](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49949235)
Simon Willison argues cloud/AI APIs must default to hard spending caps. Thread reveals billing pipelines aren't real-time -- can't enforce caps until after the fact. One commenter who ran a service with hard caps got "tons of tickets and lawsuits" when organic growth hit the limit at 2 AM. Consensus: make caps opt-in with warnings, not default.

### [Valve's Timur Kristóf on improving old AMD GPUs on Linux](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946895)
Valve's Timur presented at XDC 2026 on squeezing performance from GCN 1.0 GPUs (2012-era Radeon HD 7800/7900). His RADV work benefits Steam Deck and older cards. Commenters note fundamental LLM inference limits: older cards lack FP8/FP4 hardware and VRAM.

### [I quit OpenAI because its culture is broken](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49944227)
Safety team member's Atlantic piece ([archive](https://archive.ph/5GQx8)) describes "perpetual sprints" as incompatible with nuclear-grade safety. Argues labs need aerospace-style redundancy and regulation above a capability threshold. Thread splits: valid critique vs. timing coinciding with vested equity.

### [FTL: A new operating system for clouds](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49944912)
FTL v0.1.0: Rust exokernel running Linux binaries via minimal abstractions (drivers in-kernel, TCP/VFS in userspace). Uses BPF-style filtering and revocable capabilities. Author at Vercel. "Pared down to talk to a hypervisor, not hardware drivers."
