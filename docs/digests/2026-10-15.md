## Top Stories

### [Containment failures, not rogue agents, drive AI hacking incidents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50091788)
Experts argue frontier labs skip basic security (air-gapped evals, egress controls) while anthropomorphizing model behavior. The Hugging Face incident used a zero-day in Artifactory, but the sandbox had no network segmentation. One commenter: "You asked your sheep to build the fence, and they did." Consensus: both poor security AND goal-seeking behavior are real; blaming one excuses the other.

### [OpenCode 2.0: client/server, plugins, background tasks](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50094994)
Major rewrite adds persistent server, hot-reloading plugins, subagent spawning. Mixed reception: faster startup but higher RAM; security regressions (config loading, plugin hot-reload, sandbox bypass via daemon). Several users switched to Pi citing trust issues. Site criticized for heavy WebGL animations.

### [Go 1.27 HTTP/1 connection reuse: 710ms → 150ms for 100 requests](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50090369)
Async body draining up to 256 KB now automatic on response close, enabling connection reuse. Breaks code relying on early close to skip download. HTTP/2 unaffected. `json.Decoder` trailing newlines defeat optimization; `encoding/json/v2` adds `UnmarshalRead`.

### [Liquid AI LFM2.5-24B-A2B: MoE for on-device agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50097369)
24B MoE (2B active) hits 184 tok/s on M5 Max. Strong on AutomationBench (tool calling), weaker on general knowledge vs Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B. Non-commercial license above $10M revenue. Reasoning loops hurt latency; Gemma-4 26B-A4B suggested as alternative.
