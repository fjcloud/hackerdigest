## Top Stories

### [Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50589618)
Lean community formally verified a GRH proof using AI-assisted formalization. Multiple independent checkers with consistency proofs validated the 250k-line formalization. Thread consensus: if it holds, biggest math day since Wiles. One mathematician: verification shows it's worth the effort to understand the proof and its new ideas — humanity's next step.

### [Pill to treat pancreatic cancer hailed as 'biggest breakthrough in decades'](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50582069)
Daraxonrasib (KRAS inhibitor) doubled median survival from ~6.7 to 13.2 months in metastatic pancreatic cancer. First meaningful new treatment in decades. Signal strong because trials enroll late-stage patients; earlier use with surgery could yield durable remission. One oncologist: this is the worst the treatment will ever be.

### [Detonating a pile of lead azide](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50588982)
US Army plans open-air detonation of 10,000 lbs of degraded lead azide in Iowa — recrystallized into unstable form. Discussion is a masterclass in hazmat engineering: why burial risks triggering detonation, why containment tents vaporize, how lead dispersal compares to leaded gasoline emissions. Explosives expert: "horrific hazmat problem" with no safe solution.
