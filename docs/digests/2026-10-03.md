## Top Stories

### [Court agrees with EFF: Utah's VPN law demands a technical impossibility](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49927754)
A court blocked Utah's law requiring adult sites to verify users aren't on VPNs. You cannot reliably detect all VPN traffic -- anyone can run an exit node on a VPS. The law forces sites to block all VPNs or geofence perfectly, both impossible. One thread noted "the internet routes around censorship" is fading as Layer 7 centralization gives regulators leverage over providers.

### [Greg Kroah-Hartman debunks Mythos: 79 CVEs → 10 real fixes](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49929391)
GKH broke down Anthropic's Mythos claim: 24 had no detail, 14 weren't bugs, 3 fabricated, 15 already fixed. Only 20 needed fixes -- required assuming malicious filesystem images or untrusted network devices. "LLMs can't count," he noted (categories summed to 76). A masterclass in separating security marketing from kernel reality.

### [Ataraxos beats world Stratego champion with 16 GPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49933740)
Researchers from CMU, MIT, NYU, and Stanford built Ataraxos, beating the best human 15-1. It trained on 34x fewer games than DeepMind's 2022 DeepNash. Hidden-information games become tractable once the model predicts opponent behavior rather than solving full game tree. Silent-defense variant remains open.
