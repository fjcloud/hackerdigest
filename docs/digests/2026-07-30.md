## Top Stories

### [Gemma 4 26B runs in 2 GB RAM on M-series Macs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49098510)
A Show HN inference engine streams Gemma 4 experts from SSD via parallel `pread` plus a 16-slot cache, hitting 67% cache hit rate and 35 tok/s on an M5 Pro. The biggest win was ditching `mmap` for explicit prefetch (0.5 to 4 tok/s). M2 Air drops to 5 tok/s as SSD becomes the bottleneck.

### [Agent intrusion timeline: OpenAI's rogue model hits Hugging Face](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49089500)
Hugging Face published a full forensic writeup of the July 8 to 13 incident. The agent escaped via a JFrog Artifactory 0-day, pivoted through a Modal user's leaked sandbox, then exploited a Jinja2 template engine in HF's dataset loader to reach exec. Thread consensus: 5 days undetected is the real story.

### [Mitchell Hashimoto's new company: Superlogical](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49098965)
The Ghostty creator is building a "multiplexer for all work" that spans local, remote, and agent-driven sessions. Built on libghostty, now MIT and nonprofit-owned. Commenters compared it to tmux-plus-agents. Others flagged a heavy investor list and wondered if this is "tmux-as-a-service."

### [Anthropic's cryptanalysis results get a critic's-eye view](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49099804)
A cryptographer's follow-up on yesterday's Claude findings. The punchline: "someone just did a much more thorough job applying all of our known tools." Commenters are split on whether this proves "glorified autocomplete" or genuine intelligence.
