## Top Stories

### [MS Paint and Photos invisibly watermark AI-edited images with a GUID](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421158)
Microsoft embeds an invisible GUID into images touched by its AI tools, even with local models. The watermark ties to prompt, device, and user. The thread consensus: this isn't about labeling AI output, it's user tracking. As one commenter noted, the GUID resolves to a person on Microsoft's side, not just a model.

### [Xiaomi's XRing O3 CPU matches Apple single-threaded](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49420873)
Built on TSMC N3P with ARM C1-Ultra cores, hitting ~3,945 single-thread on Geekbench, on par with the M5 iPad. The result pressures MediaTek and Qualcomm in China. Sustained thermals and perf-per-watt in an actual phone remain the open questions.

### [IPFS Shipyard winding down after Protocol Labs pulls funding](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421489)
The team behind Kubo, Helia, Boxo, libp2p, and the public gateways shuts down September 30. The IPFS project itself is not ending, but the shift to individual grants leaves public infrastructure stewardship unclear. The thread notes Cloudflare and Brave already stepped back too.

## Projects & Tools

- **[PicoMQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421806)** -- Kafka-like durable streams over plain HTTP, backed by S3. Single binary, ~100 MiB/s per stream, ~250ms durability ACK via shared WAL. Useful for serverless event pipelines without Kafka ops.
