### [Java4Shell: RCE in OpenJDK Base64 decoder](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50538240)
Heap buffer overflow in AVX-512 VBMI-optimized Base64 decoder (CVE-2026-114938) affects OpenJDK 17.0.6+ on x86-64 with AVX-512. Exploit requires specific heap layout and low-latency network proximity — not drive-by. Mitigations: `-XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics` or `-XX:UseAVX=2`. One engineer reproduced corruption in 4,000 iterations using a canary buffer.
