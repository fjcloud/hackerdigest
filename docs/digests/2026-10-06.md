## Top Stories

### [Cloudflare launches Web Search API](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49963171)
Proxies Exa, Linkup, Ceramic.ai via AI Gateway. Pricing varies 28x ($0.25–$7/1k). Ceramic's terms forbid storing results -- breaks agent workflows. Thread: "bot protection as a service, then monetizing the allowlist."

### [Reflection AI announces Beam: 501B open-weight MoE](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49969183)
23B active params, 100M RL rollouts on 10.5K GB300s. Weights "this month" -- early access only. Trails DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. Demo claimed a puzzle was "days old"; it appeared on LessWrong in August 2025. A $4B US lab framing itself as the Western alternative to Chinese models.

### [ChatGPT forges cartoonists' signatures on fake cartoons](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49971846)
Image model reproduces signatures as visual texture. Artists call it impersonation: "they didn't dress up like me." Right-of-publicity may stick where copyright struggles. Model has no concept of "signature" as identity.

### [Opus 5.5 agents propose magnetic semiconductor candidates](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970667)
Agents ran DFT simulations, surfaced two candidates. One likely unsynthesizable; the other known since 1999. A PhD in magnetic materials tore apart the physics. Consensus: hypothesis generation, not discovery. Vals.ai is an evals platform with marketing incentives.

### [Apple and a hacker's future](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49962857)
Full Disk Access changes break agent workflows on headless Macs. Author's Mac Mini compromised via exposed VNC (port 5900) because TCC prompts forced screen sharing. TCC attaches to process-tree root (Terminal), not the binary. Gruber: Apple engineers bypass these restrictions internally.
