## Top Stories

### [NetBSD 11.0 ships with RISC-V and MicroVM kernel](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136736)
First NetBSD release with a RISC-V port, plus a MICROVM kernel that boots in roughly 10 ms and continued support for vintage hardware like VAX. Commenters framed NetBSD as the de facto home for oddball platforms where Linux has dropped support.

### [Lean kernel soundness bug enabled a fake Collatz disproof](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49137060)
An AI-assisted repo "disproved" the Collatz conjecture in Lean, but the proof exploited a kernel bug in nested inductive types, not math. The cleanest reply: the proof was unsound and Lean simply accepted it. Reviewers stressed verified proofs are only as trustworthy as the verifier.

### [Go 1.27 leans further into generics and SIMD](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49140218)
Adds new methods on generic collections, SIMD support in the standard library that already speeds up map iteration, and Android MTE compatibility. Thread pushback focused on identity: one longtime observer argued generics-enabled Go no longer reads like the simple language it once was.

### [Seedance 2.5 pushes AI video past 30 seconds](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49138302)
ByteDance's model maintains coherence across full scenes with reference-image prompting, and the demos look like real ad footage. Skeptics noted there is no clean path to weights outside China. One commenter argued the bigger story is marketers now drive creative directly, with studios relegated to finishing.
