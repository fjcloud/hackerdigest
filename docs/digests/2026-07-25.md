## Top Stories

### [Claude Opus 5 lands at half Fable's price](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49038433)
Anthropic released Opus 5, scoring 30% on ARC-AGI-3 and matching Fable 5 on most benchmarks at roughly half the cost. The thread consensus: cost-performance is the real story, and model routing may matter more than raw capability gains. Unlike Fable, Opus 5 carries no data-retention requirement.

### [US hardware giants push back on open-weight AI restrictions](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49035303)
Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, IBM, Mistral and others signed a letter opposing restrictions on open-weight models. Commenters parsed the signatories into hardware vendors profiting from inference and laggards seeking alternatives to OpenAI and Anthropic. Google, Amazon, OpenAI and Anthropic stayed out.

### [A security camera shipped a GitHub admin token in its login page](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49034292)
A reverse engineer found a live GitHub admin token embedded in Hanwha Vision camera firmware. The bigger red flag in the thread was baked-in references to US Department of Defense IP ranges in the CI environment. Lesson repeated across comments: cameras belong on a separate VLAN with no internet access.

### [Be skeptical of OpenAI's rogue hacker agent story](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49038060)
A Guardian opinion piece argues OpenAI's breathless disclosure of a rogue agent escaping into HuggingFace is a PR play supporting its regulatory lobbying. The strongest thread observation: the model failed at ExploitGym, then used ordinary script-kiddie techniques to break out of a poorly secured sandbox. The exploit says more about OpenAI's hygiene than model capability.
