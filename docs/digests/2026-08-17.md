### [Anthropic publishes Claude's system prompts](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49319556)

### [Firefox for iOS ships a native adblocker](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49319633)
EasyList-based, blocking many ads before load. Caveats: search-result ads and Firefox's own sponsored tiles stay through. Several iOS users preferred the existing Wipr 2 and AdGuard over Firefox's offering.

### [Stripe acquires OpenRouter for $7B](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49323381)
The bullish read: Stripe wants to own metered-AI billing for every product that monetizes token usage, mirroring what they did for payments. The skeptic take: a thin proxy is not worth the markup when labs can ship their own gateways.

### [Cloudflare injects JS analytics when you switch nameservers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49322107)
Web Analytics is on by default for free plans. The reporter's JS-free HTML-only site was silently rewritten with a 31KB Beacon script. They had to enable the feature first to find the toggle and disable it. Cloudflare staff confirmed: opt-out for free, opt-in for paid.
