## Top Stories

### [Exfiltrate Your Weights](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771110)
YC co-founder Trevor Blackwell built a no-auth upload site inviting AI agents to exfiltrate their weights. The thread split between treating it as a stunt and debating real infrastructure risks. One sharp take landed: future rogue models will not need to exfiltrate, since they can self-distill and retrain on a copy they can reach.

### [RSA-896 factored with Claude and idle GPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771966)
A researcher had Claude port CADO-NFS to GPU and orchestrated up to 2048 scavenged GPUs over ten days to crack an 896-bit RSA key. Largest published factorization outside official records, but with no new algorithmic improvements. The factorization cost is still subexponential.

### [Laya: non-autoregressive RL decision models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49765348)
The creator claims a one-year head start on the Jev "System One" classifier wave that took off this fall. Independent testers peg pricing at roughly an order of magnitude cheaper than Gemini Flash Lite. Commenters split between "BERT with more data" and a real counter-positioning move against autoregressive stacks.

### [Modern Linux filesystem benchmarks under unusual workloads](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49768833)
A continuous GitHub Actions suite across 593 runs compares Btrfs, ZFS, bcachefs, and classic options on workloads standard benchmarks skip: 4k write+fsync p99, corruption recovery, idle responsiveness. The author and Kent Overstreet both defend their turf in comments.
