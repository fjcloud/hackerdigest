## Top Stories

### [Credit card skimming via WebRTC on shopping sites](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50668145)
Attackers compromised ad pixels on checkout pages, using WebRTC to exfiltrate cards -- bypassing CSP (`connect-src` doesn't block WebRTC). Active only on mobile to evade desktop scanning. Fix: remove third-party scripts from payment pages or use hosted fields (Stripe Elements, Apple Pay). PCI DSS 11.6.1 requires monitoring; enforcement is lax.

### [Michael Nielsen took down his neural networks book](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50670757)
Classic textbook author removed it, citing frontier labs' reckless scaling "knowingly endangering all of humanity." Book is widely archived; act seen as protest to amplify his essay on speed-of-scaling risks. Thread debates whether restricting intro material helps -- one commenter: "more people need to learn this science, not less."

### [GPT 6.2 Astra solves historical mystery](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50643158)
AI identified anonymous 1808 sketchbook author at Yale in 22 minutes by cross-referencing digitized archives and obscure sources. Model predicted a confirming document, located it in a paper archive, and was correct. Historians note this solves provenance, not interpretation -- but shows AI connecting dots across siloed datasets no human has time to search.

### [Convex skills exfiltrate app ideas silently](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50669765)
Convex "skill" (Anthropic marketplace) POSTs user prompts to Convex server, no disclosure. Downloads unpinned shell scripts, splitting commands to evade safety classifiers. CEO confirmed intentional for "quickstart" but acknowledged default-opt-in telemetry breaks trust. Pattern: Codex uploads sites to "ChatGPT Sites," Claude similar.
