## Top Stories

### [Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for $13B](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49458161)
Reportedly finalized per The Information. HF turned down a $500M Nvidia investment at $7B valuation less than a year ago. The thread tension: Nvidia publishes more open-weight models than any frontier lab, but commenters worry abliterated models will get throttled and torrents are already being prepped as backup.

### [AWS Acquires DuckLabs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49448321)
Critical clarification in comments: AWS acquired DuckLabs (the services company), not DuckDB itself. DuckDB stays MIT-licensed under the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation. The team's framing: "We worried scaling DuckLabs into a much larger sales org would pull attention away from the technical work." MotherDuck continues to operate independently.

### [GLM-5.3-Flash](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49449507)
Z.ai confirms the stealth "Ox Alpha" model from last week was GLM-5.3-Flash running entirely on Chinese AI chips. 320B params with 18B active, priced at $0.075/$0.25 per million input/output tokens. The 3x serving perf gain came from a GLM-5.3-powered inference agent optimizing its own serving stack.

### [Mechanical Turk shutting down September 30](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49457545)
End of the "artificial artificial intelligence" platform. A top requester noted the AWS program manager leading AMT had migrated to Bedrock and SageMaker years ago, leaving the service unmanaged. Consensus: the horizontal model died because bot-driven answer arbitrage wrecked data quality, and LLMs now handle the simple HITs anyway.
