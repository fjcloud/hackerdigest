## Top Stories

### [Microsoft 365 outage leads to chlorine gas poisoning of 15 hotel guests](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50509411)
A German hotel's chlorine dosing system overfed the pool; its only alert was email via Microsoft 365. During the Nov 13 outage, the alarm went unseen and 15 guests were injured. Thread exposes a safety-critical failure: no local audible alarm, no flashing light, no dead-man switch -- just email. One engineer: "Decades of 'root cause analysis' have ruined our ability to think about safety holistically."

### [The MacBook Pro's OLED PWM problem](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50506719)
Apple's tandem OLED dims at 240 Hz PWM -- below IEEE 1789's low-risk threshold. Sensitive users report eye strain and nausea. Scope traces show 240/480 Hz components; deepest modulation remains 240 Hz. No firmware fix; only workaround is 100% brightness. Chinese phones now ship 2000+ Hz PWM.

### [Claude Code reverse engineered my 1994 Jeep's ECU](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50463399)
An enthusiast used LLMs to decode his Jeep's ECU protocol, build a custom fuel map, and pass California smog via a $3 Arduino. The LLM wrote the reverse-engineering code, generated animations, and scripted the video. Commenters see a "right to repair" revolution but warn the fix likely masks a mechanical issue, and that LLMs will soon lock down ECUs further.

### [Universal XSS in uBlock Origin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50513160)
uBlock's element-picker injects a "create rule" button into page DOM. A malicious site can hook the fetch call, steal the extension's unique UUID (a persistent tracker), and execute arbitrary JS in the extension context. Patched in 1.75.4. The fix -- move UI to browser chrome -- is harder under Manifest V3.
