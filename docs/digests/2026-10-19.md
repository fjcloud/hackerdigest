## Top Stories

### [Someone behind the software](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50142573)
Linear's Karri Saarinen argues great products carry the maker's intuition -- something AI "software factories" erode by encouraging additive feature sprawl without unified vision. Thread highlights: one commenter cited Peter Naur's "Programming as Theory Building" -- when AI writes the code, the theory isn't yours. Another noted solo developers may now have an advantage: cohesion is easier when one mind holds the whole vision.

### [Hybrid Reasoning Agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50145337)
Together AI released paper and open weights for agents that reason in continuous latent vectors before emitting text. Claims reduced token usage with maintained performance. Commenters note this resembles "continuous chain of thought" research; safety concern: latent reasoning removes the only practical window for monitoring model intent.

### [macOS 15.8.2 Breaks OpenGL](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141909)
Release candidate breaks OpenGL on common hardware. Apple's response: "please keep trying to reproduce this for us" -- asking the reporter to re-test every build. Thread captures broader frustration with Apple's software quality decline. Irony: Apple deprecated OpenGL in 2018 but kept it working via Metal; now a maintenance update breaks it.
