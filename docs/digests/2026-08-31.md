### [Creepy Crawlies: AI scrapers hammer kernel.org](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49491791)
The git.kernel.org maintainer documents a year-long losing battle against residential-proxy scrapers, with legitimate traffic at just 2% of total. Commenters agreed proof-of-work doesn't scale, since a $5K ASIC miner is 1M times faster than an iPhone at SHA-256.

### [QubesOS arbitrary code execution via copy-to-VM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49496918)
The dom0 copy tool passed attacker-controlled filenames straight to `system()`, an old-school CWE that grants full dom0 compromise. Thread consensus: this should be enforced structurally, not by code review.

### [Haiku R1/beta6 released](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499867)
New beta brings live-updating menus on modifier changes, USB audio work, and a Go runtime port. A commenter noted the AI-vibe-coded PPC fork they can't upstream under Haiku's no-AI-code policy. The project remains famously low-velocity.

### [Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49502611)
Sander Dieleman's deep dive on why CDLMs didn't take off and where they might still matter. Top reply argued the real shift was RLHF and post-training, not the generative paradigm, so the question is whether diffusion fits into a post-trained regime.
