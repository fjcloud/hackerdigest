### [Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49537553)
Google's newest Flash model ties Opus 5 Medium at one-fifth the price, including on DeepSWE. The launch glitched when the blog post briefly 404'd. The thread noted lower reasoning levels are a better signal of capability than headline scores, and Google's monthly cadence is compounding.

### [Meta releases Muse Spark 1.3](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49541256)
The coding-focused model hits the top of DeepSWE for the cheapest no-training tier. A 20x-cheaper contributor tier at $0.10/$0.20 per M tokens lets Meta train on data. Thread consensus: this is the first major lab to attach a dollar value to user data, and the transparency is overdue.

### [Fable 5.1 generates an interactive 3D Union Square](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49541458)
A developer walked San Francisco's Union Square in a single 2-hour agent run, costing about $33 API and 8M tokens. Geometry is built from OpenStreetMap and USGS 3DEP primitives via Three.js. The author noted code-generated worlds sidestep the mesh-generation topology and texturing pain.

### [Google avoids an ad tech breakup](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49537131)
A federal judge imposed conduct remedies rather than structural separation, despite findings that Google's ad exchange used anticompetitive tactics. The ad exchange is a shrinking revenue slice but still central to control of third-party display ads. One comment noted big tech now pre-games antitrust via compliance teams staffed by former regulators.
