## Top Stories

### [Android may soon restrict on-device ADB](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49045159)
Google is weighing a change to block apps from connecting to ADBD on the same device, citing attack chains where proxyware reaches the local debug port. The article authors note this effectively bans Shizuku and similar rootless tools. Commenters were blunt: an ADB toggle bound to localhost would solve the underlying threat model, but Google's framing treats the user as the threat.

### [Open-weight AI is having its Kubernetes moment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49048034)
An ex-Mesosphere founder argues open-weight Chinese labs are doing to closed labs what Linux did to Unix vendors. The strongest thread pushback: k8s worked because it ran on anything, including spare servers in a closet. Frontier inference needs data-center GPUs, so the analogy strains. Several commenters noted DeepSeek 4 runs their coding sessions for under $15 a month.

### [Cloudflare will start blocking Googlebot in September](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49052564)
Sites that opt to block AI-training crawlers will also block Google search, because Googlebot shares infrastructure with Gemini training. New defaults will also block AI training and agent traffic on ad-supported pages. Operators wanted pay-per-crawl to actually be available; the waitlist button still does nothing.

### [Debian debates four competing LLM policies](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49050859)
Proposals on the table range from outright ban to soft discouragement to allowed-with-disclosure to accept-AI-for-Debian-specific-work. The most-cited comment predicted losing a third of contributors if the stricter options pass and called the disclosure model the only realistic choice. No one offered a workable detection scheme for LLM-written code.
