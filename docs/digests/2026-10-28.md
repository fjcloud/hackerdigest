## Top Stories

### [Ghostty 1.4](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50261998)
The release notes cover the GUI, but libghostty is the story: the embeddable terminal core now builds for Windows, Android, WASM and freestanding targets, with 90% less memory per idle terminal. JetBrains shipped it across its IDEs (12.5M users), and Neovim has verbally accepted it pending work. Creator says a 1.0 ABI freeze is months out.

### [Genie 4](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50261319)
DeepMind's world model now generates interactive scenes with audio and dialogue, gated behind AI Ultra. The thread audited the demos: WASD hints that do not map to movement, a castle wing that vanishes when the camera pans, a marble bust that ignores momentum. These model video games, not physics.

### [Python's experimental JIT to be removed](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50257419)
The JIT authors withdrew PEP 836 after the steering council pressed on maintenance, tooling breakage and unclear success metrics. Measured gains were around 10% on macrobenchmarks, and the Microsoft-funded Faster CPython team has dispersed. One reviewer argued a runtime optimization should never have needed a language PEP.

### [Tailscale SSH: Investigation and remediation update](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50264857)
Confirmed privilege-escalation bugs let a user already authorized over the tailnet act beyond their configured local account, potentially reaching elevated privileges. It is not unauthenticated RCE, but Headscale is affected because the flaw lives in tailscaled. Real exposure is setups that grant SSH without root.
