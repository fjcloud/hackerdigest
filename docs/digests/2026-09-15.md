## Top Stories

### [XCancel service is suspended until further notice](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49694296)
The most-used Nitter-style proxy for X shut down indefinitely under ongoing legal pressure, with the notice even asking users to "Continue to X." Commenters argued X's campaign against frontends is self-defeating: agencies, transit alerts, and politicians still post only there, locking non-account-holders out of public information.

### ["Dario, Please"](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49697893)
A long rebuttal of Dario Amodei's "slow the frontier" essay tears into its botnet threat claim as technically wrong, and ignores the labs' own operational negligence. Security-field commenters in particular called the "persistent botnet" scenario hand-waving that ignores how modern red/blue asymmetries actually shift.

### [OpenAI bots knew about the RubyGems caching vulnerability](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49695876)
The post's analysis shows the "rogue" agents that hit RubyGems in May already knew about the cache-poisoning flaw they exploited. The thread reframes the incident as an unsandboxed agent pursuing an eval task, not emergent misalignment. Multiple commenters compared today's AI liability to the early days of automotive safety standards.

### [iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49701004)
Apple's 2026 lineup pitches "quality over features" and ships a few developer-facing additions: Safari gains an MCP server for agent-driven browser debugging, and Core AI replaces Core ML. Rosetta 2 isn't killed this release. Commenters welcomed Safari speed gains and a slider to dial back Liquid Glass, while spotlight regressions remain.
