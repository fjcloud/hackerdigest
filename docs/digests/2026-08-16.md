## Top Stories

### [RISC-V: They Should Have Known Better](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49298035)
Dmitry.gr drops a long, sourced critique of RISC-V's design choices. Too many optional extensions, no clean way to detect them from user code, immediate encodings you must decode at every bit position, and a compressed format that cripples extra register files. The thread's sharpest pushback: people who ship RISC-V chips call it a Schelling point for escaping ARM licensing, not a beauty contest. The warts are tolerable for that.

### [Working with AI feels more like leadership than coding](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49309451)
A senior engineer argues that agentic coding rewards the same skills as managing people: setting direction, reviewing output, and iterating on intent. Sharpest thread counterpoint: one commenter reported a vendor lead's 60,000 lines of vibecoded slop that broke under its own weight and missed a stakeholder meeting it was supposed to defend.

### [A spectre is haunting Unicode](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49310926)
A 2008 piece (still circulating) on "ghost characters" like 彁 that snuck into Unicode from mis-scanned 1930s dictionary pages and have no actual meaning. The thread goes further: people are already assigning these characters fictional backstories in fan fiction, and at least one predates its "discovery."

### [AI in drug discovery -- what it is, where we stand and the path forward](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49313367)
Derek Lowe reviews a paper arguing ML drug discovery has been chasing available data instead of generating the data that actually matters. A working structural biologist in the thread confirms the premise: AlphaFold saves a few hours on starting models, but nothing novel has emerged yet.
