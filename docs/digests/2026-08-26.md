## Top Stories

### [X kills Nitter and XCancel with cease and desist](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437283)
X Corp sent C&Ds to both Nitter and XCancel within hours, citing Texas Harmful Access and the Lanham Act. Threats target the maintainer personally and the GitHub repo is now archived. The thread framing: xAI wants to lock down its dataset, and these frontends were the last way to read X without an account.

### [Apple ships M6 and M5 Ultra with up to 512GB unified memory](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433292)
M5 Ultra hits 1.2TB/s memory bandwidth and supports 512GB unified memory. Maxed-out Mac Studio crosses $30K Canadian with 16TB storage. Thread consensus: Apple is now the credible home for serious local LLM inference, even if Nvidia still wins on raw tokens per second.

### [OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip claims to beat Nvidia Blackwell](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434378)
SemiAnalysis details OpenAI's custom inference ASIC, taped out in under 16 months on Broadcom co-design, claiming better efficiency than Vera Rubin at FP4. The strongest thread skepticism: speculative decoding is applied to Rubin's numbers but not OpenAI's, and the comparison is engineering sample versus shipping silicon.

### [Firefox 157 ships JPEG XL by default; Chromium following](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437946)
Both browsers now rely on jxl-rs, the Rust decoder Google Research built after Mozilla challenged them. A side-thread notes jxl-rs overtook the libjxl C++ reference on a relevant performance dashboard two months ago. After years of limbo, JPEG XL looks set to land across the web platform.

### [C2PA cameras don't survive contact with reality](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439499)
A researcher roots a Google Pixel and rewrites the C2PA manifest to attribute AI-generated images to the camera's sensor signature. The strongest thread pushback: C2PA was designed for publisher-signed provenance (BBC-style), not consumer trust of photos. The "is this real" question is probably unsolvable technically.
