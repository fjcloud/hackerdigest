## Top Stories

### [Discovery Loop: Jeff Dean and team leave Google](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184960)
Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le form a public-benefit corporation aimed at automating experimental loops in science and ML, with Google as an investor. The thread's sharpest objection: automating discovery sounds compelling until you remember E. coli still takes 20 minutes to replicate.

### [Demis Hassabis moves to Chair; Jeff Dean departs Google](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184755)
DeepMind reorganizes with Demis stepping from CEO to Chair and Alphabet Chief Scientist, while Koray Kavukcuoglu takes operational lead. The thread reads it as research culture losing to shipping pressure; GOOG dropped 5% on the news.

### [Cloudflare OS: an open platform for agents and work](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49182996)
A Sandstorm-inspired framework where every user gets their own per-document app instance, isolated enough that security teams can let non-developers vibe-code. The "OS" branding drew groans, but the per-document isolation model is the real contribution.

### [Atlassian Rovo Exfiltrates Data, Bypassing Controls](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49185983)
Prompt injection through uploaded files leaks data via Rovo's URL retrieval, even with web search disabled in admin settings. Commenters noted PromptArmor has run the same exploit against every major agentic platform; only Anthropic-style URL allowlists hold up.
