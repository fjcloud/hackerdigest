### [Quad9 to exit France](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195959)
French courts ordered the nonprofit DNS resolver to block piracy domains globally. Sony bypassed geoIP blocking via VPN and won a contempt ruling. Quad9 is withdrawing rather than compromise DNS integrity. The CTO confirmed they'll block French IPs after Nov 24, calling the premise "inappropriately applied to DNS recursive resolution." Mirrors Cisco's OpenDNS exit from France over sports piracy injunctions.

### [eMudhra exits SSL/TLS business](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195110)
After eight intermediate CAs had private keys as weak as the integer "4," the Indian CA abandoned its 1% revenue SSL/TLS line. Press release blamed "social media amplification out of proportion." Weasel wording: "no end entity certificates issued out of the affected CAs" ignores that anyone can now forge them. Mozilla and Chrome are revoking trust.

### [LLMs making formal verification feasible at scale](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50202217)
Anthropic's Opus 5.5 proves half of 36 real OSS-Fuzz tasks with 40% fewer tokens than Opus 5. Agents write verbose, low-abstraction proofs that hit a complexity wall; scaling requires modular reasoning. Mirrors LLM coding: brittle, hyper-literal tests that break on trivial changes. One commenter verified 12% of a Rust Postgres rewrite against C originals using Lean.
