### [UK forced to admit secret encryption order to Apple](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50562113)
Investigatory Powers Tribunal ruled the UK must acknowledge a Technical Capability Notice served to Apple. Order contents remain classified. Apple withdrew Advanced Data Protection from UK rather than comply. Commenters debate whether "no one, not even Apple" marketing survives — ADP remains intact in US, but UK precedent weakens trust globally.

### [OpenSSL buffer overflow in EVP_EncodeBlock (CVE-2026-121481, CVSS 9.8)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50569226)
Base64 encoding overflow in OpenSSL 4.x when input exceeds ~1.5GB. CVSS 9.8 drew skepticism: exploit requires attacker to feed multi-GB untrusted data directly into EVP_EncodeBlock, which few protocols allow. One commenter called CVSS "a Ouija board"; bug is in code written in the last year, not legacy cruft.
