### [Run Qwen 3.8 Flash Next (125B) on consumer hardware (RTX 4090) at 100T/s](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49953495)
Strata runs Qwen 3.8 Flash Next (125B MoE, 6B active) at 60-120 tok/s on a 4090 via 2-3 bit quantization. Thread debates whether Q2/IQ3 quants are "lobotomized" or useful -- several report beating Qwen 27B at Q6 on coding benchmarks. Key insight: MoE models suit consumer hardware because only active experts need VRAM.

### [Turn off Apple Intelligence on macOS 27 and reclaim disk space](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49957116)
Script disables Apple Intelligence, recovers ~12 GB. Discussion becomes a referendum on macOS enshittification: forced AI, no opt-out, 256 GB base SSDs, normalization of `curl | bash`. One commenter listed 60+ background daemons as bloat; another noted Apple's privacy marketing exempts their ad network.

### [Improper redaction reveals Google Data Center water/electricity usage](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49957068)
