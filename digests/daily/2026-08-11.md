## Top Stories

### [Meta's Muse Glimmer: 30B open weights for always-on agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49241679)
Dense 30B with multi-token prediction, Apache 2.0 licensed, and pre-quantized for 24-32GB consumer GPUs. Commenters note Meta is benchmarking against Qwen 3.6 27B from a generation back, while the Apache license is a real step up from Llama's restrictions.

### [Exploiting System Management Mode with a very long interrupt](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49245491)
A slow instruction from a userland process can stall SMM's cross-core rendezvous, letting a core enter SMM after another has already left. The author frames the architectural tension: removing the timeout risks hangs, raising it kills many-core performance.

### [Parametron: a 50s Japanese computer using neither transistors nor tubes](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49241846)
Eiichi Goto's 1954 logic device leveraged nonlinear parametric oscillation in two ferrite cores. Engineers in the thread note parametrons were a dead end almost from the start: 10-15kHz operation could not scale against the transistor's exponential roadmap.
