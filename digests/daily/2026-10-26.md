## Top Stories

### [Anthropic files its S-1](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50242939)
Revenue is $67B annualized with 45% QoQ growth and the first positive gross compute margin. The $80.7B loss is mostly non-cash convertible-note marks. The recurring risk: $505B in forward compute commitments against a business with no obvious moat.

### [A ten-year-old CNN beats frontier LLMs at skin cancer](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50233779)
An Inception v3 fine-tuned on 68k dermoscopy images beat GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 on 200 held-out lesions. ML engineers pushed back: the CNN trained in-distribution, the LLMs got one shot with no tools. The buried number matters more -- the CNN caught 77 of 100 unsafe lesions at its default threshold versus 96-97 for the LLMs.

### [IBM CEO says open weights are good enough on-prem](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50238088)
Self-serving, since IBM sells the hardware, but the thread largely agreed. The sharpest reply did the arbitrage math: DeepSeek prices its API at roughly a ten-month hardware payback, so self-hosting only wins if you can match a frontier lab's GPU duty cycle.

### [The State of Context Engineering](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50235780)
Why agents get dumber as context grows, and what practitioners actually do about it: compaction, offloading, sub-agents, strategic forgetting. One commenter added the missing dimension -- dependency length. Related facts placed far apart in the prompt hurt more than raw token count.
