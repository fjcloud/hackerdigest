### [Two-tier encryption in the UK](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49828731)
Apple disabled Advanced Data Protection for UK iCloud users rather than build a backdoor. Baseline 14 categories (Keychain, Health, Messages) stay E2EE; 9 others (Backups, Photos, Notes, Drive) revert to Apple-held keys. Workaround: switch Apple ID region to US, enable ADP, switch back -- though eligibilityd may re-check GPS/SIM. DEF CON 34 research showed baseline E2EE leaks keys without ADP.

### [Fearless SIMD v1.0](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49800085)
Portable SIMD crate hits 1.0 after 7 years. Write once, compile to SSE/AVX/NEON/WASM on stable Rust. Used in production: `memchr-n` beats `memchr` for multi-byte searches; `PhastFT` moved off nightly `std::simd`. Thread discusses tracking "never NaN" float invariants -- consensus: not yet, pattern types might enable it.
