## Top Stories

### [Nitter instance count grows post-takedowns](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49571634)
A maintained list of working Nitter instances claims more are online now than before X's crackdown. Top reply calls the effort self-defeating -- public lists make small hosts easier to find and shut down. Private instances keep working for years regardless.

### [Isar Aerospace reaches orbit from Norway](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580369)
Germany's Isar Aerospace put a privately built rocket into orbit from European soil on its second attempt. Thread clarified the headline slightly: ESA has launched for decades, but always from Kourou. Most commenters framed this as European sovereignty play, not just a launch milestone.

### [Chrome exempts Google from user site data settings](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49581870)
Reproducible bug shows Chrome preserves cookies and storage for google.com after a manual "clear site data" pass, while every other tested domain gets wiped. Author ruled out signed-in Chrome and zombie processes. Commenters pointed out the same pattern appeared across recent Chromium versions.

### ["$60 Gaming PC" AMD BC-250 -- the HN kiss of death](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49576386)
Guide to repurposing binned PS5 APUs into a Linux Steam Machine fell over from HN traffic within hours. Worse, prices had already hit $300 before the post went live. The silicon unlocks extra CPU and GPU cores and trades blows with the official Steam Machine.
