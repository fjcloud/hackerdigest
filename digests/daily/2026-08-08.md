### [DeepSeek V4 Flash](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49214008)

### [Oracle bans AI-generated code from OpenJDK](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49213754)
OpenJDK's interim policy rejects external AI-generated contributions over reviewer burden and license risk. Sharpest thread read: Oracle needs to retain the option to sue others for AI-washing, so accepting slop into its own flagship would undermine that. Rust has adopted similar rules.

### [Making Postgres 300x faster for analytics](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208535)
pgrust closes most of the ClickHouse gap via batch execution, operator fusion, and SIMD. Differential fuzzing has caught ~20 bugs in upstream Postgres, including a floating-point quadtree edge case where the center point was neither above, below, nor equal to a query point. AGPL with commercial dual-license.

### [A year of fighting scrapers on a 1.5M-page site](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49211386)
About 99% of traffic is bots. One operator logged Claude-SearchBot fetching 205,000 pages while sending exactly 1 referral. Another's logs show an "anthropic-ai" UA probing for SSH keys and service-account JSON. Anubis proof-of-work is bypassable in milliseconds via CUDA.

### [2027 memory capacity is reportedly sold out](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49207236)
HBM demand has cornered DRAM through 2027, with Micron noting HBM3E uses ~3x the wafer supply per bit of DDR5. Consumer prices climb as capacity pivots to AI. New fab capacity will not ease the market until at least 2029.
