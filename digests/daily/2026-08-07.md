## Top Stories

### [AMD buys Taalas, betting on model-specific silicon](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201970)
AMD acquired Toronto startup Taalas, whose compute-in-memory ASIC reportedly serves Llama 3.1 8B at roughly 17,000 tokens per second by embedding weights in silicon. HN's useful caveat is that this fits older, good-enough models better than fast-churning frontier systems. Cheap, private inference for classification and high-volume agents may be the real market.

### [GitHub Actions outage exposes a control-plane dependency](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49198302)
GitHub Actions and Pages spent much of August 6 degraded, with self-hosted runners also unable to obtain jobs because scheduling and webhooks remained on GitHub's control plane. One commenter cited Actions growth from 500 million minutes per week in 2023 to 2.1 billion this week. Self-hosted runners do not remove GitHub's availability risk.

### [Humans fail the approval prompt as an agent security boundary](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49195468)
A game based on 409,000 decisions found players missed roughly one-third of malicious agent commands. The thread challenged the study's methodology, but its stronger consensus stands: repeated prompts train users to click through. Sandboxing, explicit file and network capabilities, and invariant-based containment are more credible than asking humans to inspect a command firehose.
