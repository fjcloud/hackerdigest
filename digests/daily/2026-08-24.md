## Top Stories

### [Qwen 3.8 27B reverse-engineers a license check in 30 minutes](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49407507)
A small local model pulled off what most frontier models wouldn't: breaking a non-standard RSA license-check in a binary, including an online activation gate, end-to-end. The thread's sharpest observation: recent models have become unusually persistent. They verify their work and iterate until a byte-level hash matches, even when an early attempt looks plausible. Persistence is now the axis of capability gain, not raw reasoning.

### [JIT Compiling Code in 5μs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49406387)
Postgres contributor ships `pgrust`, a copy-and-patch JIT for PostgreSQL that compiles PL/Rust functions in microseconds versus LLVM's millisecond-scale overhead. Postgres disabled its LLVM-based JIT by default earlier because the compile latency killed the wins. Commenters pointed out the same approach powers WebKit's B3 and eBPF. Full LLVM is overkill for many JIT use cases.

### [Why your local LLM feels dumber than it is](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49402232)
The thread is more valuable than the article. The top reply identifies that gguf files often strip the chat template metadata, silently falling back to ChatML and degrading output. Second-place offender: sampling defaults in UI wrappers like Ollama differ from vendor-recommended settings, so users benchmark against numbers that were run with official configs. That likely explains many reports of `gpt-oss 80B on H100` being "surprisingly dumb."

### [Malware infects Android-based automotive head unit firmware](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49408550)
Kaspersky finds residential-proxy malware pre-installed via official OTA updates on cheap Chinese aftermarket Android head units. The article downplays risk, but commenters noted head units hold location history, call logs, and contact lists, plus always-on cellular connections. That's ideal botnet real estate, and the obvious next move is lateral propagation via Bluetooth-paired phones.
