## Top Stories

### [Mistral ships a 3B multimodal moderation model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49171268)
Shieldstral runs a yes/no classifier over arbitrary policy prompts, sidestepping hardcoded rules baked into frontier models. Commenters framed it as a deliberate strategy. Mistral cannot match frontier scale, so it is carving out vertical, cost-sensitive workloads where purpose-built small models win.

### [City of Munich funds an external developer to maintain libexpat](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49176606)
The city's Open Source Sabbatical program pays outside contributors to work on projects the city depends on but does not own. The thread noted Munich's long Linux migration history and surfaced ongoing interest in city-funded maintenance as a model for sustainable open source work.

### [Eight Myths on Software Engineering and GenAI](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49176830)
An ACM Queue piece argues the headline metric that developers only spend 14 percent of their time coding caps AI's productivity gains. Several engineers pushed back hard. Cheap code lets teams prototype alternatives rather than perfect specifications, and the article's Amdahl-style math does not match how they actually use agents today.

### [Workday sunsets Flowise, citing coding agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49176920)
The low-code agent builder will stop development roughly a year after Workday's August 2025 acquisition. A competing vendor argued in-thread that visual workflows still serve non-developers who need deterministic, reviewable pipelines, even as coding agents absorb the developer segment.
