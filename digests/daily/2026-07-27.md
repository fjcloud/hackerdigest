## Top Stories

### [EU commission tries again to kill cookie banners](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49057175)
The proposal lets users set tracking preferences at the browser level and skip per-site banners. The thread's sharpest observation: Google killed an earlier version in 2024 via Council lobbying, with EUR 40-50B in ad revenue at stake. Skeptics pointed out the previous "Do Not Track" failed because no law required sites to honor it.

### [Atlanta activist charged after GrapheneOS phone wipes at border](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063022)
A duress PIN entered at CBP's request wiped the device. Federal charges stack destruction of evidence onto a man already flagged over Cop City activism. Commenters split on strategy. The smarter move was refusing the PIN and letting them seize it, since the visible wipe pattern itself telegraphs the duress feature.

### [PGSimCity visualizes PostgreSQL as a living city](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063754)
A vibe-coded 3D simulator where Postgres internals become buildings, transactions become traffic, and queries become visible flows. The author credits 3.86B tokens of Claude Opus 5 work. The thread asks for slower pacing and interactive query tracing. One engineer floated the same idea for Fly.io's deployment system using Factorio.

### [Proof automation is finally cheap enough to use](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49062291)
Imperialviolet uses LLMs to write Lean 4 proofs for zstd, finding the bottleneck has shifted from proof effort to spec-writing. The hard problem remains. An LLM cannot tell you what correctness even means, only verify the spec you give it. One commenter reported Claude reproduced a week of HOL Light work in fifteen minutes.
