## Top Stories

### [Tim Peters has died](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50450488)
Python lost its "Uncle Timmy" -- author of Timsort, the Zen of Python (`import this`), and core contributor since the 1990s. The thread is a moving archive of his patience and humor. He was suspended by the PSF in 2024 over a CoC dispute many called unjust; he returned to work on Python anyway.

### [My AI agents found an unmapped Maya site?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50452455)
A Google SWE used 48 agents to scan 1,200 km² of LIDAR data and reported a potential discovery. INAH replied no site was registered. The author later acknowledged the site appeared in a 2025 paper -- the agents missed the prior literature. Thread debates whether "vibe archaeology" accelerates discovery or floods experts with unverified leads.

### [AWS Emulator](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50456631)
AWS released an official local emulator of their APIs, built almost entirely by AI agents (~30K commits in weeks). Written in Rust, it uses Smithy specs and runs Lambda functions in-process. Skeptics note 6,500+ "things-that-do-not-work" issues, no IAM policy emulation, and 100GB build requirement.

### [Critical RCE in Apache HTTP Server](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50462177)
CVE-2026-119618 in mod_http2 (2.4.55–2.4.70): improper synchronization causes heap corruption. Discovered independently by Anthropic and Google. Red Hat confirms reliable RCE on default config. Mitigation: `a2dismod http2`. One commenter reproduced in 7 minutes for $0.02.
