### [Reverse engineering my Coway air purifier to ditch the cloud](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50474959)
Engineer reverse-engineered Coway purifier's Bluetooth protocol for local Home Assistant control. Device downloaded root CA cert over plain HTTP -- trivially MITM-able. LLMs accelerated the tedious RE work. Thread reveals growing trend: hobbyists using AI to liberate IoT devices from vendor clouds, though manufacturers will soon use LLMs to lock devices down more effectively.

### [$11.4M in Nvidia hardware vanishes during shipment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50487864)
Six pallets of Nvidia GPUs (~150 cards) disappeared in transit Oakland to Texas. Third reported GPU heist this year; previous incidents saw hardware routed toward China. Commenters debate whether $11M matters to Nvidia (0.01% revenue) or signals targeted theft. Logistics discussion: commercial freight terms often transfer risk at warehouse handoff; insurance prices in expected shrinkage.
