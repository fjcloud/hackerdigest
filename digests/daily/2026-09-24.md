### [Italian parliament votes for return to nuclear energy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49819221)

Targets energy sovereignty (73% imports), but commenters note: no commercial SMRs exist, cooling water scarce in warming Mediterranean, economics need subsidies. One Italian called it a "penalties business model" -- contracts canceled, state pays out.
