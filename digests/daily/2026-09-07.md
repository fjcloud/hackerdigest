## Top Stories

### [Nitter and XCancel resume service after legal advice](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49588988)
The maintainer says legal advice lets the Nitter project and major instances continue, but host operators' exposure remains unclear. HN notes that the repository is not the service, and account-backed scraping leaves operators exposed to X bans and terms-of-service claims.

### [Asahi Linux on M3](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)
Asahi Linux has reached Apple's M3 generation, extending bare-metal Linux on Apple Silicon through another reverse-engineering cycle. M3 remains partial, with GPU, Thunderbolt, and stable HDMI and sleep still missing, so it is not a drop-in desktop.

### [GrapheneOS Overhauled Default Apps and Secure Clipboard](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49590512)
GrapheneOS is replacing AOSP Messaging and adding a secure clipboard, with MLS-based RCS planned later. The maintainer says LLMs are used mainly for code review and more tests, not generated production code, a better fit for security work.

### [It took a year to ship WebAssembly in Anubis](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49590611)
Anubis now runs memory-hard proof-of-work in WebAssembly, with a JavaScript fallback for older or restricted browsers, after a year of compatibility work. It is an economic brake, not a bot wall: extensions and GPU solvers can lower the cost, but the goal is to make crawling expensive.
