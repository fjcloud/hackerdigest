## Top Stories

### [EVE Online finally moves to Python 3](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433328)
CCP is migrating 2.4M lines, 16 years after the last upgrade (Python 2.7 in 2010). They rejected asyncio and built their own scheduler inside the now-open-source Carbon Engine. Commenters split between respecting the technical feat and bewilderment that this is still happening in 2026.

### [Tencent open-sources Hy4 preview](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49492632)
A 770B-total / 49B-active MoE with 1M-token context under Apache 2.0. Heavy OpenRouter traffic already, helped by a 5% cache-read cost versus the 10-20% most providers charge. Hy4 contributed to its own training by running experiments whose results fed subsequent rounds, an early recursive self-improvement loop.

### [California exempts open source from age-verification law](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49495372)
The amendment excludes anyone distributing software under terms permitting copy, redistribute, and modify. Linux, BSDs, and most OSI projects qualify, but macOS and iOS do not. Thread consensus: a patch on a bad law, not a fix, and the "Linux exemption" framing in the press underplays how much the original bill overreached.

### [Samsung's Processing-in-Memory at Hot Chips 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49487341)
Samsung is still pushing PIM despite decades of false starts. One commenter called it "the aura of fusion power." Killer app framing: LLMs, where a fully PIM-equipped eight-socket box could push bandwidth past tens of TB/s and break the memory-wall on matrix multiplies.
