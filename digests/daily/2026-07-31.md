## Top Stories

### [Krebs: streaming sticks ship with residential-proxy malware](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49112744)
Cheap Android TV boxes come pre-flashed with botnet software that sells your home IP as a proxy and spoofs mobile clicks on AI-generated sites. The Bitsight author says Synthient's tracker lists close to 1,000 affected models. Commenters are split over who is liable since Amazon and Best Buy keep stocking them.

### [GitHub stacked PRs exit preview](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49112232)
Stacks let you split a feature into a chain of dependent PRs and merge them in order, mirroring Graphite and ghstack. GitHub staff confirmed the launch spans Actions, merge protection, and the mobile app. Thread consensus: the AI-PR era forced this since reviewers can no longer eyeball 1,000-line diffs. Some users flagged rough edges around squash-merging entire stacks.

### [GPT-5.6 Luna drops 80% in price](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49112867)
Output pricing on the cheapest tier falls to $1.20 per million tokens, citing 20% lower serving cost from kernel work plus 15% better token efficiency. Commenters noted Sol (high) still beats Luna (high) on time-per-task. One commenter called it "the dialup-to-broadband transition."

### [GCC steering committee publishes AI policy](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49108685)
Contributors must demonstrate understanding of changes above 15 lines, and LLM-generated commits need an "Assisted-by:" trailer. The policy cites copyright risk since purely AI-generated output is not copyrightable under US law, threatening GPL enforceability. The LWN comments thread is unusually heated.
