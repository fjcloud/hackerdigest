## Top Stories

### [Malicious Rust crate `arrayref` runs a build-time payload](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49374269)
The maintainer's account was compromised and a proc-macro release shipped with hidden exfiltration code, hitting downstream builds. The thread's recurring point: Cargo still has no sandboxing for build.rs, and the long-discussed `minimum-release-age` RFC has languished for years, leaving "update everything" as the de facto mitigation.

### [GitHub's August 17 outage postmortem](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49378957)
Nearly eight hours of downtime across core workflows after load balancers in Central US saturated, with a latent VS Code retry bug amplifying recovery traffic 10x. The commits graph tells the real story: monthly commits doubled from 1.4B to 2.9B since April, and the thread consensus is that AI-generated sludge is overloading infra faster than Microsoft is willing to throttle it.

### [AliExpress WebAudio fingerprinting breaks Bluetooth multipoint](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49372583)
A silently streaming tone keeps the PC-to-headphone link permanently active, so the user's phone calls never come through. The site sends a known waveform through WebAudio and fingerprints the device from how the pipeline distorts it.

### [Mojo is now open source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49348079)
Modular (now Qualcomm) released the compiler under Apache 2, ending the closed-source era that blocked most adopters. Commenters frame it as Qualcomm's pitch to GPU and accelerator vendors: bypass Nvidia's CUDA moat by plugging into the Max stack instead of building a full software bring-up from scratch.
