### [The Goose Programming Language](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50224100)
Wouter van Oortmerssen releases Goose: multiple compile-time data stacks instead of heap. No borrow checker, no UB, compiles to C. Carmack: "Parsing often 100x slower than needed." Enables direct memory-mapping and flat pointer-free trees. Critics called AI-generated docs unreadable; author acknowledged AI wrote most code.
