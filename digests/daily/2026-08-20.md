## Top Stories

### [Stripe acquires OpenRouter](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49364559)
The LLM routing proxy joins Stripe at a reported ~$7B. Thread consensus: routing is a thin layer over commodity APIs, and the real moat is negotiating provider rates and aggregating token spend. One engineer's framing: a proxy is just a percentage on top of every operation.

### [Go 1.27 released](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49365405)
Generic methods land without explicit type arguments, plus a new stdlib uuid package, SIMD support, and Russ Cox's faster float parsing. Commenters expect a flood of PRs swapping github.com/google/uuid for the built-in.

### [Google moves GPL Pixel kernel source to Google Drive](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49364745)
GrapheneOS reports Google stopped pushing kernel driver tags to AOSP and now requires a form plus manual review, with weeks-long delays. Commenters debate whether this crosses into GPL violation or stays as malicious compliance, but agree the friction is deliberate.

### [SondeHub: joke domain to geopolitical warfare](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49360015)
A weather-balloon tracker recounts becoming entangled with the Ukraine war, Russian sonde vendors, and the US Department of War. Sharpest thread moment: a citizen-science project ends up routing sensitive wartime requests under unexpected constraints.
