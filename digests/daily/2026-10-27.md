## Top Stories

### [Microsoft offers mass buyouts for roughly a quarter of its workforce](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50245524)
Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees -- a soft layoff dressed as choice. Terms reach 39 weeks of base pay plus six months of COBRA, and far fewer than 25% are expected to accept. As one commenter noted, Microsoft earned $134B in net income over the past year, so this is cost-center reclassification, not a crisis.

### [Three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science, and physics](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50251344)
OpenAI published three results -- all-dimension Kakeya, a subexponential #3-SAT algorithm, and a QCD existence proof -- but the ETH disproof is the one shaking computer science. The bound is roughly 2^(n / sqrt(log n)): still superpolynomial, and reduction overhead means it does not prove NP is subexponential. A SAT-solving expert called the algorithm "galactic," and Scott Aaronson moved his P=NP estimate from 3% to 5%.

### [Cross-request KV cache exposure in the GLM-5.3 Workers AI model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50252159)
Cloudflare disclosed that a misconfigured GLM-5.3 endpoint leaked 155 responses across requests. It shipped a 1,048,576-token context window against a 681,280-token KV cache pool, and an SGLang bug served uninitialized memory instead of erroring. The RCA is candid. Shared-tenant inference has isolation classes most teams have not audited.
