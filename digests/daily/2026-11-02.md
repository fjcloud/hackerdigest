## Top Stories

### [Postgres 19 is late because so many more bugs are being found now](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50312408)
Robert Haas attributes the delay to LLMs making it vastly easier to find decades-old bugs in Postgres' 30-year codebase. One commenter reported hundreds of candidate bugs from a summer of AI-assisted hunting, including serialization anomalies and CVE-worthy issues. Discovery is now cheap; verification and triage are the bottleneck.

### [Lenovo's 'encrypted' fingerprint USB is just an unencrypted SD card in a case](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50326099)
The ThinkPlus FU100, sold as AES-256 hardware encryption, contains a plain SD card; the fingerprint reader merely toggles the USB connection. An AI-assisted follow-up found backdoors and hardcoded keys in other "secure" USB products. The flaw was found in August 2025 but only surfaced now because the product was China-only.

### [Mathematics has changed. Let's act like it](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50328251)
A guest post on Terry Tao's blog argues math has shifted from craft to technology, and that choosing worthwhile problems now matters more than producing proofs. Commenters counter that AI proofs are long, ugly, and bypass peer review, and point to the Simons Foundation's $15M fund for mathematicians navigating the AI era.

### [HackerTube: YouTube built from videos recommended by HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50319510)
Karpathy built a YouTube front-end ranked by videos recommended in HN comments. The cost breakdown is the interesting part: 504M input tokens, 90% of it Sonnet labeling comments, while the app itself took about 1M tokens of Opus. HN's admin revealed in the thread he is finally building a JSON API for the site.
