## Top Stories

### [PipePipe: NewPipe hard fork implementing SponsorBlock](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49842764)
NewPipe rejected SponsorBlock, calling sponsorship ethical advertising. PipePipe forked to add it plus faster YouTube-breakage fixes. Thread has desktop alternatives (FreeTube, Grayjay, mpv+yt-dlp) and iOS options (Yattee, YouMod). One commenter summarized NewPipe: "We don't consider advertising problematic -- it's the privacy-invasive systems that are evil."

### [ASML sold 'absolutely nothing' in Europe in 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49844663)
Zero EUV orders this year; TSMC's Dresden fab runs 12nm, not leading-edge. Commenters debate regulations, energy costs, and missing industrial policy. Intel's €30B Irish fab proves advanced manufacturing *can* work in Europe -- with green-energy commitments and subsidies. The EU Chips Act missed packaging entirely, a gap the US CHIPS Act addressed.

### [DeepSeek Elastic Compute (DSec)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49859112)
380K concurrent Firecracker sandboxes on 160 EPYC nodes (30K cores, 250 TB DRAM), 5K creates/sec. 131 authors -- particle-physics scale for CS. Commenters call it "serverless for agents" and note AI agents are becoming the largest cloud compute consumers.

### [How to keep enjoying programming with LLMs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49854875)
Haskell Discourse thread splits between "LLMs removed drudgery" and "I've lost the ability to think." Multiple engineers reported skill atrophy -- one couldn't architect a small project without AI after months of delegating. The "third-person effect" is strong: everyone thinks *they* use AI responsibly but colleagues don't. Japanese kanji literacy declined when IMEs let people recognize but not write characters.
