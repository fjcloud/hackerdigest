### [OpenAI claims Navier-Stokes solution, accused of stealing research](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49613262)

### [Anthropic researcher resigns over superintelligence risk](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49619227)
An employee quit publicly saying both labs are racing toward self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives. Replies split between treating it as a sober warning and as IPO-aligned marketing.

### [AlphaGenome Atlas maps every possible human DNA variant](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49611251)
DeepMind precomputed AlphaGenome's predictions across all 9 billion single-letter variants as a 1-petabyte dataset. Genomics specialists noted AlphaGenome itself offers little improvement over Borzoi, and a mutagenesis study on a virus showed models still miss most variant effects.
