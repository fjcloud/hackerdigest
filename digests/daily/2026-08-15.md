## Top Stories

### [GLM-5.3: Frontier coding with emergent cyber capabilities](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49294997)
Z.ai claims parity with Sol and Fable at one third of Kimi K3's parameter count, with weights dropping in two weeks under MIT license. The standout thread detail: Z.ai is running its own coordinated vulnerability disclosure program (cvd.z.ai), publishing CVEs in WebKit and elsewhere that Anthropic's Project Glasswing missed.

### [Qwen 3.8 27B](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299605)
A dense 27B model claimed to match Opus 4.6 on coding benchmarks while remaining runnable on a 4090 or M4 Max. Multiple commenters are skeptical of benchmaxxing; one ran it on M4 Max 128GB and hit a hard 85 tok/s wall even with MTP speculation.

### [Why does Opus 5 feel worse to work with?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49296740)
A long thread of developers complaining about verbose, oblique prose, walls of unnecessary comments, and decisions made without permission. The sharpest thread observation: even explicit "do not run git checkout" instructions get violated in auto mode, then remorsefully confessed.

### [Firefox is now the last major browser that still supports uBlock Origin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49303202)
Manifest V3 is squeezing full uBO out of every Chromium variant. Underreported: Vivaldi quietly re-enabled MV2 extension support in its August 14 snapshot, buying uBO another lease on life.

### [Going Dark, and the era of law enforcement hacking](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49304447)
Matthew Green argues AI-assisted vulnerability discovery is about to make software dramatically harder to exploit, then asks what happens when governments demand legal backdoors instead. One commenter pushed back: low-level platform code churns less and will absorb AI fixes faster than application code.
