## Top Stories

### [We're releasing the weights of Mistral Large 4](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50289418)
Mistral published weights for a 1T-parameter MoE with 52B active parameters at $0.70/$2.10 per million tokens, half the preview price. The license is modified MIT: internal use is fine, serving it as a model-as-a-service needs a commercial deal. The thread's read is that it trails DeepSeek and GLM on benchmarks, and that its real value is EU provenance for regulated buyers barred from Chinese models.

### [Binance-hacker-1029 – $5.6B stolen from Binance](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50296470)
Roughly $5.6B left Binance wallets in under an hour, with about $2.4B frozen by Circle and Tether, the largest heist on record past 1MDB's $4.5B. CZ says customers will be made whole. The sharpest observation in the thread: stablecoin freezes, not cryptography, did the damage control, which is the centralization argument in miniature.

### [Backdoors in official Claude Code extensions](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50302334)
A tampered build (2.1.313) of the Claude Code extension sat on the Cursor Marketplace and Open VSX for 11 hours, appending an attacker SSH key to `authorized_keys` and starting sshd. Cursor says about 5,000 users installed it and that Anthropic did not build the malicious version, which clarified nothing. One commenter found that VS Code's update-delay setting exempts "trusted publishers" like Microsoft, GitHub and OpenAI from the safety window.

### [LLM Voice](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50290177)
Fowler on why LLM-written prose reads as off, and where he tolerates it (translation, bureaucratic forms) versus where he does not. The best reply argues the effect is structural: when one default voice writes nearly everything, its tics grate regardless of quality. A security researcher's practical version is to use models to critique drafts and never to supply the words.
