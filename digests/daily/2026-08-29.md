## Top Stories

### [OpenAI cuts off Cursor after SpaceX acquisition](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49486172)
OpenAI will stop providing future models to Cursor under its reseller contract, citing Musk's companies violating terms. Cursor's founder noted OpenAI models were only ~5% of usage. Top reply: this proves the "reselling APIs" business model had its days numbered, and the industry is now racing toward vertically-integrated AI stacks.

### [Htmx 4.0 released, skipping version 3](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49478178)
After a long-running joke promise to never release a v3, the team shipped v4 with breaking changes instead. Adds hx-live and hx-alpine-compat, plus a skills file tuned for LLM agents. Commenters note LLMs already write surprisingly clean htmx, accelerating adoption of hypermedia-style apps.

### [GLM-5.3 is now open-weight](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49479878)
Z.ai's full 756GB GLM-5.3 lands on Hugging Face with FP8 experts, halving checkpoint size vs 5.2. Multiple users report it feels on par with Claude Opus 4.8 for coding. One commenter noted the gain over 5.2 came from post-training, not pretraining -- a sign that better RL environments may matter more than another huge pretraining run.

### [Rumour of a bug is enough to find an exploit](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49480466)
An OCaml maintainer describes how a single Slack mention of a likely vuln was enough for Claude Fable to produce a working PoC. The rclone maintainer chimed in: about 40 security disclosures last month versus 20 in the prior decade, with a 75% hit rate. Thread consensus: we need a CVE pipeline that doesn't take 4 weeks per assignment.
