### [Windows 11 collects data on coding agents and dev tools you use](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50524736)
Optional diagnostic data (default on) tracks `claude.exe`, `codex.exe`, `cursor.exe` paths, uploading to Microsoft unless opted out. Thread: expected but default-on and re-enabling after updates frustrate users. Group policy steps to disable it.

### [Optimizely compromised, served malware on Zoom, GitLab, CNN, BBC](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50524767)
Attackers gained write access to Optimizely's script infrastructure, injecting a skimmer stealing crypto/credit cards plus iOS exploit kits to hundreds of sites. Not CDN poisoning -- Optimizely's own domains served it. Strict CSP and SRI would have contained it; marketing tag managers make CSP impractical.
