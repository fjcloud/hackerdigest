## Top Stories

### [Local Wikipedia](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50202285)
Wrapper around Wikipedia's "Special:Nearby" with manual location input. Thread reveals the built-in feature is unusable for many (ISP geolocation wrong, no override). English Wikipedia sparse outside Anglophone regions; local-language Wikipedias have more coverage. Alternatives: wiki-map.com, wikimap.wiki, OsmAnd.

### [Kimi K3.1 Pro](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50212065)
Moonshot's 3T model scores 55 on Artificial Analysis (4th, beating Astra/Fable). Open weights Nov 4. Intentionally removed cyber-offense data from post-training to avoid regulation. Pricing ~30% cheaper than K3. Subscription quotas painful: users burn 5-hour limits in 30-45 min.

### [iPhone untethered jailbreak via PDF exploits](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50211474)
Calif (Thai Duong, lcamtuf, Tavis Ormandy, Stefan Esser) demos iOS 27.0.1 jailbreak: PDF parser RCE → sandbox escape → kernel execution → persistence across reboot. Disclosed ~100 bugs to Apple in 2026, no bounties. Lockdown Mode blocks iMessage vector. Technical details coming weeks.

### [Twelve years of distributed SQLite](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50217500)
Philip O'Toole traces dqlite → rqlite → D1, LiteStream, cr-sqlite. Most "distributed SQLite" is Raft-backed single-writer, not true multi-writer. rqlite praised for control-plane simplicity. D1 revealed as thin wrapper over Durable Objects -- Cloudflare devs admit Durable Objects directly is often better.
