## Top Stories

### [Kernel.org vs. the bot scrapers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49491791)
The git.kernel.org maintainers document a year-long losing war: legitimate traffic is just 2% of requests. Anubis proof-of-work initially blocked two-thirds of bots, but 33% now solve it. Top reply: a $5K ASIC miner is 1M times faster than an iPhone at SHA-256, so PoW cannot be the long-term answer.

### [QubesOS RCE via copy-to-VM error reporting](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49496918)
`qvm-copy-to-vm` passes an attacker-controlled filename straight to `system()`, granting full dom0 compromise. The danger was documented in 2020 and still shipped. Top reply: this should be enforced structurally, not by code review.

### [Haiku R1/beta6 released](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499867)
Sixth beta brings live-updating menus on modifier changes, USB audio work, and a Go runtime port. One user reverted to a vibe-coded Focusrite driver after kernel panics on boot, reigniting debate over Haiku's no-AI-code policy.

### [Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49502611)
Sander Dieleman asks why CDLMs never took off and where they could still matter. Top reply: the real paradigm shift was RLHF and post-training, not generative modeling, so the open question is whether diffusion fits a post-trained regime.
