## Top Stories

### [Toyota, Nissan, Honda standardize parts to lower costs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50596133)
Japan's three largest automakers will standardize steel specs, plastics, and wiring color codes to compete with Chinese EVs. BYD makes 75-85% of parts in-house; Japanese firms were slow on pure EVs. Thread debates whether standardization lowers prices or just raises margins. Irony: Japanese makers historically avoided this, but supply-chain shocks forced their hand.

### [What Happened to Silicon Valley?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50602947)
O'Reilly: SV became an extraction machine (75-80% of IPOs unprofitable). System optimizes for shareholder value, not human flourishing. Best insight: capitalism is a machine built on changeable rules (tax, bankruptcy, corporate law). Shift traced to 1970s Friedman doctrine. Google's dual-class structure meant Page/Brin couldn't blame shareholders — it was personal moral decline.

### [Perplexity and Intelligence](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50601525)
OpenAI publishes log-perplexity vs. capabilities chart claiming smooth improvement. Comments dismantle it: test set is internal codebase (skewed by more code training), perplexity doesn't capture RL gains, GPT-4.5 is a discontinuity — larger but worse than o1/o3. "Perplexity tracks base model IQ; RL is where real capability jumps happen."

### [Free TV network from Internet Archive videos](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50606192)
Spelunkr.tv streams curated Archive.org as linear TV — Bob Ross, Computer Chronicles, 90s anime — with period commercials and scrolling guide. Top request: M3U/XMLTV export for IPTV clients. Archive encourages such frontends. One commenter runs a local version with ErsatzTV and custom fake ads.
