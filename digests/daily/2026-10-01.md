## Top Stories

### [Gemini 4 Argon](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49913571)
Google announced Gemini 4 Argon with SOTA benchmarks and aggressive pricing ($2/M in, $10/M out, 95% cached discount). Not actually available -- only for "trusted cyber defenders" via Fairwind. Argon agents already migrating C++ to Rust at Google scale (800K+ lines for Fuchsia's Zircon kernel). Thread debates moats: "Nobody has a moat except Nvidia" drew pushback that even Nvidia's is shrinking.

### [EDG C++ front-end goes public](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49913192)
Edison Design Group open-sourced their C++ frontend (Apache-2.0 + LLVM exception) -- one of four major frontends. The catch: EDG is winding down as customers migrate to Clang. Codebase: mostly C, 1990 commit history, compiles in <10s, emulates GCC/MSVC/Clang bug-for-bug. Only implementation to ever support C++98 `export template`.

### [5x faster Edge Functions: V8 isolates to Firecracker MicroVMs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49912444)
Netlify moved edge functions from externally-hosted V8 isolates to Firecracker MicroVMs (via Unikraft) in their own edge network, cutting median latency 5x. Key insight: old isolates weren't at the edge -- requests traversed internet to a hosted service. Cloudflare Workers always ran isolates natively at edge.
