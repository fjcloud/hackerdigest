## Top Stories

### [I accidentally logged hundreds of thousands of phone calls to military bases](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49387570)
A 19-year-old registered `enum.org.uk` and accidentally started receiving ENUM (e164.arpa) DNS queries mapping phone numbers to VoIP endpoints. Calls to military bases on Diego Garcia flooded in, exposing a forgotten telecom protocol still in production. The thread consensus: ENUM was meant to bypass traditional phone networks but never achieved public adoption, leaving internal telecom subscribers as its only remaining users.

### [How Nari Labs made Qwen3-TTS respond in sub-50 ms](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49389952)
Nari Labs pushed Qwen3-TTS to 34ms p95 time-to-first-audio on a single H100 and open-sourced the work. The most useful thread detail: Qwen3-TTS supports streaming input, so an LLM's tokens can be piped in mid-generation rather than waiting for full sentences, making a real-time voice agent actually feel present.

### [AI boosted homework scores, then exam scores dropped: a 27,000-pupil study](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49357530)
Homework scores rose 18% and completion time fell from 64 to 45 minutes among Chinese students using Doubao or DeepSeek, but the same cohort scored 20% lower on exams. The sharpest thread observation: AI crowds out effort rather than harming learning efficiency itself. Students who spent equal time to non-AI users did equally well on exams.

### [Claudette: a Gemini-powered filter for Claude's "BuzzFeed" prose](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49388752)
The tool routes Claude's output through Gemini to strip the rhetorical flourishes and engagement-bait phrasing Opus 5 has become known for. Commenters framed it as a striking indictment of Anthropic's product choices, with several noting they had already switched to competing models just to escape the prose.
