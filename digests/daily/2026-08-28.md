## Top Stories

### [Cloudflare saves 100TB by optimizing 1.1.1.1's DNS cache](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468083)
Cloudflare engineers halved their global DNS cache memory with Rust-level changes. They dropped unused capacity fields, swapped `Vec<u8>` for `Box<[u8]>`, and packed records flat. Commenters noted the trick is old news in C: one developer cut a 237MB blacklist to 9.5MB using a single-malloc arena.

### [Small Models Have Arrived](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49466917)
Peter Reinhardt argues cheap hosted models like Luna cover roughly 95% of what running a company actually takes. The framing: most engineering work is "token spewer" task-tending, not "IQ 180" creative leaps. Frontier models are overkill for daily use, and the takeaway for product teams is to price accordingly.

### [Luanti removed from Google Play over baseless DMCA](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49475079)
Microsoft filed a Minecraft copyright claim via Tracer.AI against Luanti, an open-source voxel platform predating Minecraft 1.0. Thread consensus: bogus DMCA notices cost filers nothing, so the pattern persists until someone prosecutes a false claim. Microsoft lost the same fight in 2023 and is back.

### ["IT Works Better in the App"](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477600)
Google, airlines, and ISPs lock users into apps that are often WebViews with worse UX than mobile sites. Top reply: an ISP removed its router's web admin entirely and forces app access full of upsells, pushing the user to buy Ubiquiti gear behind the ISP's combo box.

### [Sovereign Tech Agency invests €500k in Flatpak](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49474786)
Germany's sovereign tech fund pays Flatpak maintainers to harden desktop sandboxing on Linux. Critics wanted the money on underfunded C/C++ libraries. Defenders argue STA's role is feature acceleration, not sustained maintenance -- that comes from agencies that adopt the software.
