## Top Stories

### [Google Gemini routes personal Drive files and emails to human annotators](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50429426)
Consumer Gemini with "Connected Apps" sends Drive/Gmail data to human annotators. Workspace Gemini claims no human review, but the distinction is lost. Thread consensus: Google conflated consumer and enterprise data flows. One commenter had to disconnect apps in both Gemini and Google Search separately -- settings don't sync.

### [Payments Canada launches Real-Time Rail (RTR)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50427362)
Canada's FedNow equivalent goes live with ISO 20022, sub-2-second settlement, centralized fraud tooling. Fintechs first (Wealthsimple, KOHO); Big Five banks join by mid-2027. Unlike Interac e-Transfer, RTR settles instantly between institutions. Commenters compared rollout to SEPA Instant -- technically live years before broad adoption.

### [E-means: Outperforming k-means in one-tenth the epochs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50429734)
New clustering algorithm combines mini-batch updates with exponential moving average of centroids, achieving 10x faster convergence. One practitioner reported 146x GPU speedup adapting it to their IVF index. Skeptics note hyperparameter laws appear post-hoc fitted; baselines omit well-tuned scikit-learn mini-batch k-means.

### [Amazon saves millions replacing Sonnet 4.6 with Strands Decider](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50436640)
Internal ticket classification (~100M tickets/year) moved from Claude Sonnet to fine-tuned Qwen "decision model" at 98% lower cost. Pattern: LLMs prototype fast but lack production ROI; specialized small models win at scale. Thread noted JSON key order changing output probabilities by 0.19.
