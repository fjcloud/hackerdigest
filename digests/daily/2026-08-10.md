## Top Stories

### [Windows 11's Weather app wastes more than 1 GB of RAM](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49232138)
The built-in Weather app is a WebView2 wrapper around MSN Weather, spawning multiple Chromium processes. The thread's pragmatic fix: install uBlock Origin in Edge, then add MSN Weather as a PWA -- 130MB instead of 1GB. The most cynical comment observed the Weather team exists to juice a metric for a middle manager.

### [What Happened to HackerOne?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49238561)
A long-time bug bounty researcher walks away after a decade, citing sales-driven priorities, AI triage the company denies training on, and a co-founder reply that reads like AI slop. The sharpest thread read: a chatbot response at the tipping point irreparably damages trust in a way no rollout strategy can repair.

### [How I use LLMs to learn complex topics](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49234675)
The author has an agent build a knowledge base, then render a Rollercoaster-Tycoon-style interactive simulation of it. Many loved the technique. The sharpest critique: animations hide the complexity that makes the topic worth learning, and asking the same model to self-review does not catch its own hallucinations.
