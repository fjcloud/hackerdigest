## Top Stories

### [Why isn't the industry freaking out about DeepSeek 4.1 Flash?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50000488)
DeepSeek's 4.1 Flash delivers near-frontier coding at a fraction of the cost (sub-$1/day heavy use). Subscription users on subsidized plans see no urgency; API-paying enterprises are quietly migrating. The real threat: if "good enough" models need 10x less compute, GPU cluster loan collateral evaporates.

### [Whistle: Speech to Text in 16.9 MB](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50008427)
On-device STT model achieving usable English accuracy in 16.9 MB -- small enough for L3 cache. Supports 7 languages, runs on CPU/RISC-V, streams via WASM. Value: embed STT in apps without cloud latency or privacy concerns. 30-second audio limit noted.

### [OpenAI withdraws three mathematical results](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50002650)
Three of ~400 papers retracted after mathematicians found errors (one a sign error). Only ~42% had Lean formalizations. Debate: is this "peer review at scale" or "slop generation"? The verification burden shifted to the community while the proposer gets the credit.

### [DuckDB Ducklake](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49996149)
New open table format spec (like Iceberg/Delta) with catalog in Postgres/SQLite instead of metadata files. Engine-agnostic -- DataFusion impl exists. Still alpha (v1.5.4 has bugs, v2 parser 10x slower) but beats hand-rolled Parquet stores when working.

### [What should we tell our students?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50015236)
Terry Tao hosts a guest post addressing a math PhD student's AI anxiety. Advice: focus on "alien ideas" outside the convex hull of existing knowledge, direct AI as a compiler not an oracle, accept 40-year planning is impossible. Parallels to software engineering noted.
